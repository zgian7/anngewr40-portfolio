Portfolio Structure

Before, During, After

I reorganized this portfolio around growth instead of calendar order. The quarter did not feel like ten separate boxes to me. It felt more like a change in how I understood what writing can do: first I described food, then I learned to analyze how food writing creates meaning, and finally I revised my own work with more control over claim, evidence, voice, and audience.

Before During After Read Reflection View Evidence

Before

Food writing as memory and reaction

I came into the quarter thinking food writing was mostly about taste, preference, and personal experience. My first-day Oreo response already showed that food could carry fear, memory, and trust, but I did not yet have a strong vocabulary for explaining how those meanings were built on the page.

See the first-day writing

During

Learning to read for technique

Annotation changed the way I read. Instead of asking only what a text was saying, I started asking how it was saying it: through tone, exaggeration, structure, contrast, imagery, and the personality of the speaker. Tasker and Wendig helped me see that style can be evidence.

See annotation evidence

After

Revision as rethinking

By the end, revision became more than fixing sentences. In the Food Fight workshops, I practiced making a thesis more specific, giving each paragraph a job, explaining evidence after I used it, and making the conclusion answer why the argument matters.
