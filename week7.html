WR40 · Craft Exercise Ann Ge · May 11

S26 Show, Don't Tell Activity

This activity helped me revise summary into scene. Instead of simply explaining that food can carry memory, I practiced using sensory detail, action, and setting so the reader can experience the argument before being told what it means.

I also worked on replacing broad emotional labels with images and actions, so the draft's food scenes could become more persuasive through specific details readers can interpret.

Revision Focus

The most useful part of this exercise is turning abstract claims into concrete evidence: smell, texture, movement, dialogue, and small moments that make an argument feel lived-in.
